# 23. NEXUS

Jarvis must understand **NEXUS** as an overarching L-Code e-commerce architecture.

It may connect areas such as:

* Pricing Engine,
* SafeOrder,
* Availability Intelligence,
* procurement,
* Shoptet,
* Shopify,
* vouchers,
* other e-commerce intelligence.

However:

> Shared ecosystem does not mean everything must become tightly coupled.

Keep modules logically separated where separation improves:

* reliability,
* security,
* maintainability,
* deployment,
* testing,
* scalability.
